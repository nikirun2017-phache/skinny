Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Skinny keeps your data on your iPhone. We don't run servers, create accounts, show ads or use analytics.

What stays on your device

Your profile, the foods you log, your weigh-ins and your reminders are stored only on your iPhone. Meal photos are analyzed on the device by Apple's Vision framework and are never uploaded.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health, Skinny reads your steps, active energy and body weight, and writes the food, macros and weigh-ins you log. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your device through Skinny. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app.

Barcode and online food search

When you scan a barcode or search the online database, Skinny sends only the barcode number or your search words to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to get nutrition facts. Nothing that identifies you is sent. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Deleting your data

Delete any logged food from the Today screen, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Data Skinny wrote to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.

Children

Skinny is not intended for children under 16.
