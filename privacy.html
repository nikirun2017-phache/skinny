Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Pip Calorie Counter keeps your data on your iPhone. We don't create accounts, show ads or use analytics.

What stays on your device

Your profile, the foods you log, your weigh-ins and your reminders are stored only on your iPhone.

Meal photos

If you turn on smarter photo analysis (Pip Calorie Counter asks before the first photo), each meal photo you take is sent, together with your app language, to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude API to estimate the foods and calories. Our server does not store photos. Anthropic processes the photo to answer the request and may keep API data for a limited time under its own policies (anthropic.com/legal/privacy); it is not used to train models. If you don't turn it on, or you're offline, photos are analyzed on your iPhone by Apple's Vision framework and never leave the device. You can change this any time in Settings.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health, Pip Calorie Counter reads your steps, active energy and body weight, and writes the food, macros and weigh-ins you log. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your device through Pip Calorie Counter. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app.

Barcode and online food search

When you scan a barcode or search the online database, Pip Calorie Counter sends only the barcode number or your search words to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to get nutrition facts. Nothing that identifies you is sent. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Deleting your data

Delete any logged food from the Today screen, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Data Pip Calorie Counter wrote to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.

Children

Pip Calorie Counter is not intended for children under 16.

Data shared with others

We don't sell data or share it for advertising. The only data that leaves your iPhone is described above: meal photos (with your permission), and barcodes or search words sent to Open Food Facts.
