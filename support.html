How is my daily calorie target calculated?

Pip Calorie Counter estimates your resting burn with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, multiplies it by your activity level, then subtracts a deficit for the weekly pace you chose. It never goes below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men).

Why didn't the photo recognize my meal?

Photo recognition runs on your iPhone and knows common foods. If it misses something, use Search, Barcode or Quick add, and adjust portions with the sliders.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Open Settings in Pip Calorie Counter and turn on Apple Health. To change access later, open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Pip Calorie Counter.

How do I change units or language?

Units: Settings in Pip Calorie Counter → Units (Metric, US or UK). Language: iPhone Settings → Apps → Pip Calorie Counter → Language.

How do I delete my data?

Tap × next to any logged food. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Data written to Apple Health can be removed in the Health app.

Is Pip Calorie Counter medical advice?

No. Pip Calorie Counter gives general guidance. Check with a doctor before changing your diet if you are pregnant, under 18, or have a health condition or a history of eating disorders.
